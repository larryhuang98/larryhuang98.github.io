I am **Zhen Huang (黄镇)**, a postdoctoral researcher in computational chemistry
in the [Luo Lab](https://rayluolab.org/) at the University of California, Irvine.

I develop Gaussian-density force fields and scientific software for molecular
simulation. My work spans the polarizable Gaussian multipole (pGM) model,
the DEGAUSS softcore potential, and Gaussian van der Waals interactions,
together with their MPI and CUDA implementations in
[AMBER](https://ambermd.org/).

I also work on long-range electrostatics, energy conservation in polarizable
models, free-energy calculations, and machine-learning interatomic potentials.
